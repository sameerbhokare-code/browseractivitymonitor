Who can see my activity

People listed here can see the domains and times uploaded from your browsers, only for visits after you authorized them. You can revoke at any time.

    Authorization request

    This person will be able to see which websites (domains only) you visit and when, from every browser where you have cloud sync switched on, starting now. They will not see full web addresses, searches, page content, or anything from before now. You can revoke this at any time on this page.

    People sharing with me

    To ask someone to share with you, create a code and give it to them. They enter it in their own extension and decide whether to authorize you.
